# Lab 2 — Product Backlog (Book Club Reading Challenge & Discussion Portal)

Built from the Lab 1 requirements (FR-001 – FR-005, NFR-001, NFR-002). Story points use the Fibonacci scale (1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13).

## Epic 1: Reading Progress Tracking  (13 pts)

**Description:** Enable Club Members to set a personal reading goal, log page/percentage progress on the active book and see a progress bar toward their goal, on desktop and mobile. (FR-001, NFR-002)

| Story | As a | I want | So that | Priority | Points | Sprint |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1.1 Log Reading Progress** | club member | to log the page number or percentage I have reached in the current book | my progress is saved and I can keep track of where I am | High | 3 | Sprint 1 |
| **1.2 View Progress Toward Goal** | club member | to see a progress bar comparing my progress with my personal reading goal | I stay motivated and know if I am on schedule | High | 5 | Sprint 1 |
| **1.3 Set Personal Reading Goal** | club member | to set a target (pages or finish date) for the current book | the portal can measure my progress against it | Medium | 2 | Sprint 2 |
| **1.4 Mobile-Friendly Progress Input** | club member using a phone | the progress input and progress bar to work on a 360px-wide screen | I can update my reading from any device | Medium | 3 | Sprint 2 |

## Epic 2: Chapter Discussions & Spoiler Protection  (14 pts)

**Description:** Let Discussion Leads open a discussion thread per chapter/milestone where Club Members can comment, with spoiler-tagged comments hidden until the reader chooses to reveal them. (FR-002, FR-003)

| Story | As a | I want | So that | Priority | Points | Sprint |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **2.1 Create Chapter Discussion Thread** | discussion lead | to create a discussion thread for each chapter or reading milestone | conversation stays organised by how far members have read | Medium | 3 | Sprint 1 |
| **2.2 Post Comment in Chapter Thread** | club member | to post comments in a chapter's discussion thread | I can share my thoughts with others at the same point in the book | Medium | 3 | Sprint 1 |
| **2.3 Spoiler-Tag a Comment** | club member | to mark my comment as a spoiler | members who are behind are not shown plot reveals | High | 5 | Sprint 1 |
| **2.4 Reveal Spoiler Content** | club member | to click to reveal a spoiler, or have it shown once I mark the chapter as read | I only see spoilers when I am ready | High | 3 | Sprint 1 |

## Epic 3: Book Nomination & Monthly Voting  (20 pts)

**Description:** Let Club Members nominate books and vote once in a monthly poll created by the Discussion Lead, with a live, duplicate-proof tally. (FR-004, FR-005, NFR-001)

| Story | As a | I want | So that | Priority | Points | Sprint |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **3.1 Nominate a Book** | club member | to nominate a book with its title, author and a short reason | my suggestion can be considered for the next monthly poll | Medium | 2 | Sprint 2 |
| **3.2 Review Nomination Queue** | discussion lead | to review all nominations in one queue | I can choose which books go into the poll | Low | 2 | Sprint 2 |
| **3.3 Create Monthly Poll** | discussion lead | to create a monthly poll from the nominated books | the club can vote on the next book | High | 3 | Sprint 2 |
| **3.4 Cast One Vote Per Poll** | club member | to cast exactly one vote in the monthly poll | the next book is chosen fairly | High | 5 | Sprint 2 |
| **3.5 Live Vote Tally** | club member | to see the vote count update in real time | I know which book is winning without refreshing | Medium | 8 | Sprint 2 |
